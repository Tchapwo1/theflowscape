QUESTIONS & DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

How do I travel to the retreat in Tuscany?

The retreat is easily reached via Florence Airport (FLR, 1 hr) or Rome Fiumicino (FCO, 2 hrs). Private group shuttles are arranged directly from Florence station.

Is prior yoga experience required?

Not at all. Every morning flow and evening restorative session offers thoughtful modifications for both beginners and experienced practitioners.

Can dietary preferences or allergies be accommodated?

Absolutely. All menus are freshly prepared vegetarian meals using local organic produce. Vegan, gluten-free, and specific allergy options are gladly tailored upon notice.

What is the group size?

In accordance with The Flowscape standards, the retreat is strictly capped at 12 to 14 guests to ensure privacy and personal guidance.
