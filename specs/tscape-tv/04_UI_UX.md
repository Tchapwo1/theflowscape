# 🎨 Pillar 4: UI/UX & Visual Design Architecture — TSCAPE TV

## 1. Visual Aesthetic Direction: Architectural Cinema Gallery
**TSCAPE TV** extends **The Flowscape** dark-obsidian & linen design tokens into a focused cinematic screening experience (inspired by Criterion Collection, NOWNESS, and MUBI):

- **Color Palette**:
  - **Cinema Obsidian**: `#111613` (Theater backdrop & deep video canvas)
  - **Warm Forest Pine**: `#1e2521` (Editorial containers & cards)
  - **Linen Ivory**: `#f7f4ee` (Crisp editorial text contrast `> 12:1` on obsidian)
  - **Lotus Crimson**: `#b42d32` (Live badge, Play indicator hover, active channel pills)
  - **Terroir Ochre**: `#d4a373` (Timestamps, sanctuary badges, secondary labels)

- **Typography**:
  - **Display / Episode Titles**: `Playfair Display` (weight 400–600, italic accents)
  - **UI Metadata, Durations, Badges**: `DM Sans` (uppercase tracked letter-spacing `0.12em`, tabular numerals for durations like `04:18`).

## 2. Signature Components
1. **TSCAPE TV Brand Header Mark**: Red Lotus SVG paired with editorial wordmark `TSCAPE TV · MEDIA CHANNEL` + direct breadcrumb link back to `← Back to The Flowscape`.
2. **Cinema Spotlight Hero**: Full-width featured episode card with blurred ambient glow, video preview thumbnail, duration badge, sanctuary tag, and magnetic crimson **Watch Transmission ▶** button.
3. **Source Indicator Badges (Optional Minimalist Micro-Tag)**:
   - Displays subtle source clarity cleanly without tech clutter (e.g., `4K FILM` / `DIRECT STREAM` / `SANCTUARY REEL`).
4. **Theater Overlay Modal**: Smooth fade-in backdrop (`rgba(12, 16, 14, 0.94)`), centered responsive `16:9` player frame, Sanctuary Companion CTA bar beneath the video linking directly to that retreat's booking page!
