QUESTIONS & DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I attend as a day guest or is overnight lodging required?

Both options are available. We offer complete 3-day residential packages including luxury townhouse lodging, as well as day-pass packages for local Londoners wishing to sleep at home.

Do I need previous yoga experience?

All sessions are crafted to welcome every level, with options ranging from gentle foundational movement to advanced variations guided by top instructors.

Where in London do the retreats take place?

Our sanctuaries are situated in leafy Richmond-upon-Thames and Hampstead—both easily reachable via London Underground and Overground lines.

What style of food is served?

We partner with renowned plant-based culinary chefs to provide creative, organic, and seasonal meals. Gluten-free, vegan, and all allergy requests are comprehensively catered for.

What does joining the early access list mean?

You will receive 48-hour advance booking priority and special founder rates before each weekend retreat is opened to the wider public.
