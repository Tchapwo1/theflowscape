QUESTIONS & DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

Do I need previous yoga experience?

Not at all. Every morning flow and evening restorative session offers thoughtful modifications for both beginners and experienced practitioners.

How do I travel to the retreat in Provence?

The Luberon is easily reached by train via Avignon TGV (2h40 from Paris) or by flight to Marseille Provence Airport (MRS). We provide scheduled group shuttles directly to the bastide.

When is lavender season in Provence?

Lavender typically blooms across the Luberon and Valensole plateaus from late June through July. Our spring and autumn retreats celebrate wildflowers, mild sunshine, and the olive harvest.

Can dietary preferences or allergies be accommodated?

Absolutely. All menus are freshly prepared vegetarian meals using local organic produce. Vegan, gluten-free, and specific allergy options are gladly tailored upon notice.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

It provides exclusive 48-hour advance booking access and preferred pricing before dates are announced to the public. No deposit or payment is taken.
