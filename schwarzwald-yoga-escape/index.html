QUESTIONS & DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

How do I reach the retreat location in the Black Forest?

We are easily reached via EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg (45 mins) or Frankfurt Airport (2 hrs by high-speed ICE train to Freiburg). Private station transfers are arranged for all residential guests.

Is prior yoga experience required?

No. Our teachers provide modifications for every stage of practice—from restorative foundations to dynamic challenges.

What dietary requirements are accommodated?

All meals are organic, seasonal, and vegetarian/vegan-forward. Gluten-free, dairy-free, and all specific allergies are seamlessly handled by our private chef.

What is the group size?

To maintain intimate connection and spaciousness, our retreat is strictly capped at 12 to 14 guests.

What happens once I join the early access list?

You will receive advance notification of confirmed retreat dates 48 hours prior to public release, along with founding guest rates.
