QUESTIONS & DETAILS

A few things
you might wonder.

How do I reach the retreat in the Swiss Alps?

The retreat is easily reached via Zurich Airport (ZRH) followed by the scenic Rhaetian Railway glacier route directly into the Engadin Valley. Station transfers are arranged for all guests.

Is prior alpine hiking or yoga experience required?

No. All mountain practices and walking meditations are mindfully calibrated for all experience levels.

What dietary options are provided?

All meals are 100% organic, seasonal, and vegetarian/vegan-forward, crafted by our private Swiss alpine chef.

What is the group size?

In alignment with The Flowscape standards, the retreat is strictly capped at 12 to 14 guests.
